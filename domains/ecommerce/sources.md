- **`eu-crd`**: Distance-selling consumer rights: pre-contract information, 14-day withdrawal/return right, refund timelines.
- **`pci-dss`**: Card data handling for checkout/payment capture.
- **`gdpr`**: Personal data handling for customer accounts, order history, and marketing consent.
