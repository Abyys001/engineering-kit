## Notes

- **`inventree`**: Permissive (MIT). Inventory/parts management with light manufacturing (build orders). Cloned src/backend/InvenTree/{part,stock,order,build,company,common} — Part, BomItem, BomItemSubstitute, PartTestTemplate, StockItem, StockLocation, StockItemTracking, StockItemTestResult, Build/BuildLine/BuildItem models and their status_codes.py enums. Frontend and other backend apps were not cloned.
- **`odoo-mrp`**: reference-only (LGPL). Sparse-checkout limited to addons/mrp (the manufacturing module) from the odoo/odoo monorepo (branch 20.0), plus root LICENSE/COPYRIGHT. Covers mrp.production (production order), mrp.workorder (operation execution), mrp.workcenter, mrp.bom/mrp.bom.line/mrp.bom.byproduct, mrp.routing.workcenter, mrp.unbuild, and the stock_orderpoint/stock_lot/stock_move files bundled in that addon.
- **`erpnext-manufacturing`**: Not cloned (no-new-downloads rule); read via `gh api repos/frappe/erpnext/contents/...` at HEAD, license verified from the actual LICENSE file (GPL-3.0), not a README badge. Reference-only, GPL-3.0 code never enters the kit. Doctypes inspected: Work Order, Job Card, BOM/BOM Item (allow_alternative_item, routing, is_phantom_bom), Workstation, Production Plan, Quality Inspection, Non Conformance, Quality Action, Subcontracting Order, Maintenance Schedule/Asset Maintenance, Stock Reconciliation, Purchase Order, Supplier — and their status Select-field options.
- **`isa-95-iec-62264`**: Cited for the Equipment Hierarchy Model (Enterprise>Site>Area>Process Cell/Work Center/Work Unit) and the Production/Maintenance/Quality/Inventory Operations categories and Production Schedule/Capability models. Full normative text not accessed (paywalled) — cited for terminology and model structure only, never for a verbatim field list.
- **`b2mml`**: XML implementation of the ISA-95/IEC 62264 object models (ProductionSchedule, ProductionPerformance, Personnel/Equipment/Material/Process-Segment schemas). Cited alongside isa-95-iec-62264 for production-schedule and work-center object models; individual .xsd files were not fetched/inspected.
- **`gs1-general-specifications`**: Defines GTIN (AI 01), SSCC (AI 00), and the GS1-128/Application-Identifier barcode syntax used for part/lot/container identification and labeling. Version/date corroborated via GS1 UK and GS1 Denmark member-organization announcements; the ref.gs1.org document itself exceeded the fetch tool's size limit and was not read in full. Distinct from the already-registered std:gs1-epcis (event vocabulary) — this is the identification/barcode layer.
- **`opc-ua-iec-62541`**: Cited only for the existence and scope of the machine/shop-floor connectivity protocol (relevant to a machine_connectivity integration), not for a specific data-model detail. Individual parts were not fetched.
- **`iso-9001`**: Direct iso.org fetch returned HTTP 403; version/publication date corroborated via multiple independent secondary sources (ANSI Blog, DNV, DQS, TUV, Punyam) rather than the primary page itself. Cited for the general existence of inspection/nonconformity/corrective-action requirements (clauses on release of product, nonconforming outputs, and corrective action), not for a specific data model.
- **`iso-10012`**: Not fetched directly (iso.org blocked automated fetches); version corroborated via WebSearch of ANSI Blog and Beamex coverage. Cited for calibration/measurement-equipment traceability relevant to quality_inspections; full text not accessed.
- **`fda-qmsr-21cfr820`**: Fetched directly from fda.gov. Supersedes the former free-standing 21 CFR Part 820 Quality System Regulation text for devices sold in the US as of the effective date.
- **`fda-21cfr-part11`**: Direct ecfr.gov fetch was blocked by the site's bot-protection redirect (302 to an interstitial page); scope (electronic records/signatures equivalence to paper/handwritten signatures for FDA-regulated records) not independently re-fetched this session beyond well-established public knowledge of the rule's text and applicability.
- **`eu-mdr-2017-745`**: Not fetched directly this session; cited for the UDI/traceability requirements (Article 27, UDI system) relevant to manufacturing.traceability in the medical-device sub-sector, based on well-established public knowledge of the regulation rather than a fresh primary-text read.
- **`fda-fsma-204`**: Confirmed via WebSearch results linking directly to fda.gov and federalregister.gov; the FDA page itself was not fetched in full this session. Defines Key Data Elements (KDEs) and Critical Tracking Events (CTEs) for foods on the Food Traceability List — the sector-specific analogue of manufacturing.traceability's genealogy concept.
